# Privacy & Ethics Statement

This repository contains a sample traffic video for demonstration and validation. The video was captured in a public setting and may contain individuals and vehicles. To protect privacy, faces and license plates have not been intentionally blurred in the sample video; however, users are strongly advised to apply anonymization if reusing this data in publications or public demos.

If you have concerns about the presence of your personal information in this dataset, please contact the repository owner to request removal.

We are committed to ethical research and encourage users to follow institutional review board (IRB) guidelines and local data protection regulations.
